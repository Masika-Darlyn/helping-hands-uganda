Women & Girls Empowerment

Restoring dignity, building skills, and creating opportunities for a brighter future

Empowering Women, Transforming Communities

Helping Hands Uganda runs comprehensive empowerment programs aimed at improving the livelihoods of women and girls. We believe that when women are empowered, entire communities are transformed.

Our programs address the unique challenges faced by women and girls in rural Uganda — from limited access to education and economic opportunities to social isolation and vulnerability to exploitation. Through skills training, mentorship, and economic empowerment initiatives, we help women build independence, confidence, and hope for the future.

Our Empowerment Programs

Tailoring Skills Training

Our flagship program provides vocational training in tailoring and sewing. Young women who have dropped out of school learn practical skills that enable them to earn a living, support their families, and regain confidence.

5 sewing machines | 5 girls trained every 3 months

Through the kindness of friends abroad, a GoFundMe campaign helped us acquire these machines, which we still use today. The demand continues to grow as more girls seek training.

Mentorship & Guidance

Our mentorship programs connect young women with role models who provide guidance, encouragement, and life skills training. Participants learn about goal-setting, self-confidence, and making positive life choices.

Ongoing mentorship | Holistic support

We believe that skills alone are not enough — women need confidence, support networks, and guidance to succeed. Our mentors walk alongside participants throughout their journey.

Economic Empowerment

Beyond skills training, we help women access small loans through our SACCO program, connect with markets for their products, and develop business skills to build sustainable livelihoods.

SACCO membership | Business training

Graduates of our tailoring program can earn income making clothes for their community, selling products, and building financial independence for themselves and their families.

How It All Began

In 2022, we realized that one of the most urgent problems in our community was the lack of opportunities for young girls who had dropped out of school. Many faced a future of limited options, early marriage, or economic dependence on others.

With no resources but a deep commitment to change, we started a small tailoring skills project. At first we had no sewing machines. But through the kindness of friends abroad, a GoFundMe campaign helped us acquire five sewing machines, which we still use today. With those machines, we began training groups of five girls every three months, giving them a skill that could help them earn a living and regain confidence.

"I never thought I could learn a skill. Now I can make clothes for my family and even earn money. The training gave me confidence and hope for my future."

— Program Graduate

Today, the demand continues to grow as more girls seek training. Each quarter, we receive more applications than we can accommodate with our current resources. Our goal is to expand the program, acquire more machines, and train more women to become self-sufficient.

Why Women's Empowerment Matters

Economic Independence

Empowered women can earn their own income, support their families, and contribute to the local economy. This creates a ripple effect of prosperity throughout the community.

Better Outcomes for Children

When women are educated and economically empowered, their children are more likely to attend school, stay healthy, and break the cycle of poverty.

Reduced Vulnerability

Women with skills and income are less vulnerable to exploitation, early marriage, and domestic abuse. Empowerment provides safety and dignity.

Community Leadership

Empowered women become leaders in their communities, advocating for change, mentoring others, and building stronger social structures.

Our Vision for the Future

We dream of a future where every woman and girl in our community has the skills, confidence, and opportunities to build a life of dignity and independence. We envision:

  • A fully-equipped tailoring training center serving 50+ women annually
  • A cooperative where graduates can sell their products and support one another
  • Expanded mentorship programs reaching girls in schools across the district
  • Economic empowerment programs that help women start and grow their own businesses

With your support, this vision can become a reality. Together, we can transform the lives of women and girls — and build stronger, more resilient communities for generations to come.
