SACCO & Community Loans

Building economic resilience through community savings and microfinance

Empowering Communities Through Financial Inclusion

Helping Hands Uganda operates a community savings and credit initiative (SACCO) that supports farmers and small business owners in Bundibugyo District. This program provides small loans to help members invest in farming, expand their enterprises, and build financial independence.

Access to affordable credit is one of the biggest challenges facing rural communities. Without it, farmers cannot purchase seeds and tools, small business owners cannot expand, and families remain trapped in poverty. Our SACCO bridges this gap by providing accessible, fair loans that help members grow their livelihoods.

What is Our SACCO Program?

Community Savings

Members contribute regular savings that build a collective fund. These savings create the capital that the group can lend to members in need.

Small Loans

Members can apply for small loans to invest in farming, purchase inventory, expand their businesses, or cover emergency needs.

Financial Literacy

We provide training on budgeting, saving, and business management to help members use loans effectively and build long-term financial health.

Peer Support

Members support one another, share business advice, and hold each other accountable. This community approach builds trust and collective responsibility.

Benefits of Joining Our SACCO

Access to Capital

Obtain loans to purchase seeds, fertilizers, tools, and equipment for farming.

Business Expansion

Small business owners can access funds to buy inventory, expand operations, and grow their enterprises.

Lower Interest Rates

Our loans are offered at fair, community-friendly rates — much lower than informal lenders or loan sharks.

Flexible Repayment

Repayment schedules are designed to align with harvest seasons and business cash flow cycles.

Financial Education

Members receive training on financial management, saving strategies, and business planning.

Community Support

Join a supportive community of farmers and entrepreneurs who share knowledge and encouragement.

How the SACCO Works

1

Join the Group

Become a member by completing registration and contributing initial savings.

2

Regular Savings

Make regular contributions to build the group's capital and your personal share.

3

Apply for a Loan

Submit a loan application explaining your business or farming plans.

4

Group Approval

Members review and approve loan applications together, ensuring transparency.

5

Invest & Grow

Use the loan to invest in your farm or business, generating income.

6

Repay & Save More

Repay with interest, building your credit and increasing future borrowing capacity.

Types of Loans Available

Agricultural Loans

For purchasing seeds, fertilizers, tools, and livestock. Repayment aligned with harvest seasons.

Business Expansion Loans

For small business owners to purchase inventory, equipment, or expand their operations.

Tailoring Enterprise Loans

Special loans for graduates of our tailoring program to purchase sewing machines and materials.

Emergency Loans

Short-term loans for urgent needs like medical emergencies or unexpected family expenses.

Loan Terms & Conditions

Interest Rates

Fair, community-friendly interest rates significantly lower than informal lenders.

Repayment Period

3-12 months depending on loan purpose, with flexible schedules aligned to harvest seasons.

Group Guarantee

Members support each other's loans, creating collective accountability and lower risk.

Loan Limits

Loan amounts based on savings history and business plan. Members can increase limits over time.

Financial Accountability & Transparency

Helping Hands Uganda follows strict financial management procedures to ensure transparency and accountability:

  • All funds are held in an official organizational bank account
  • Two authorized signatories required for all financial transactions
  • Regular financial reports presented to members during meetings
  • Annual audits conducted by independent auditors
  • All loan disbursements and repayments are documented and transparent

Members can trust that their savings are safe and that the program is managed with integrity and accountability.
