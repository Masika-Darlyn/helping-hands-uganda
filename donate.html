Our Commitment to You

At Helping Hands Uganda, we take our responsibility to donors seriously. We promise that:

  • 100% of donations go directly to our programs serving vulnerable communities
  • We operate with transparency and accountability
  • All financial transactions require two authorized signatories
  • Annual financial reports are available to all donors
  • You will receive updates on the impact of your giving

Registered Community Based Organization in Bundibugyo District, Uganda. Your donation is tax-deductible where applicable.
