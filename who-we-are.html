Who We Are

A grassroots humanitarian organization born from compassion, serving vulnerable communities across Uganda

Our Identity

Helping Hands Uganda is a Community Based Organization (CBO) registered in Bundibugyo District, dedicated to restoring dignity, opportunity, and hope to vulnerable children, women, and families across Uganda.

Founded in the aftermath of the COVID-19 pandemic, we witnessed firsthand how rural communities struggled—children dropping out of school, young girls facing early marriage, and families losing their livelihoods. What began as a small gardening project sharing harvests with vulnerable families has grown into a comprehensive humanitarian initiative serving orphaned children, empowering women, and building sustainable community development programs.

Our Founder's Story

Bukama Hakim

Founder & Chairperson

My name is Bukama Hakim, founder of Helping Hands Uganda. I was born into a life that taught me vulnerability firsthand. My mother, married at just 21, faced hardships in a polygamous household and later left to protect her children. By age seven, I knew what it felt like to be displaced, to struggle for stability, and to watch a mother fight tirelessly for her children's future.

I believe deeply that human beings must help fellow human beings. Every child deserves protection. Every girl deserves education. Every vulnerable person deserves dignity.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I watched children in my village never return to school, young girls married off early, and families lose hope. I knew I had to act. Starting with a small gardening project, then a tailoring initiative with five sewing machines, Helping Hands Uganda was born. Today, we care for orphaned children, provide skills training, and work to build a future where hope is stronger than hardship.

Our Commitment to Child Protection

Helping Hands Uganda is deeply committed to protecting children from abuse, exploitation, and neglect. We operate under a comprehensive Child Protection and Safeguarding Policy that ensures the safety and well-being of every child under our care. All staff and volunteers undergo background checks and safeguarding training.

Our principles: Every child has the right to safety, education, and a nurturing environment where they can thrive.
