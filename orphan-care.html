Orphan Care & Shelter

Providing safe, loving homes for children who need them most — because every child deserves dignity and security

A Safe Haven for Vulnerable Children

In many rural communities in Bundibugyo District and across Uganda, orphaned and vulnerable children face daily struggles that most people rarely see. The loss of one or both parents often leaves children without stable shelter, regular meals, access to education, or the emotional support necessary for healthy development.

Helping Hands Uganda was established with a deep commitment to respond to these challenges by providing care, protection, and hope for orphaned and vulnerable children. The organization currently provides shelter and daily care for a group of orphaned children who live under the supervision of our dedicated caregivers.

The Challenge We Face

Overcrowded & Unsafe Shelter

One of the most urgent challenges currently faced by the organization is that the house where the children are staying is old, overcrowded, and structurally unsafe. The building does not provide the level of protection and comfort that growing children need for healthy development. During harsh weather conditions, the children remain vulnerable due to the poor condition of the structure.

Emotional Trauma & Instability

Many orphaned children carry deep emotional wounds caused by loss, neglect, or traumatic experiences. Without proper support, they face increased risk of dropping out of school, experiencing neglect, suffering emotional trauma, or being exposed to exploitation and early labor.

Vulnerability to Exploitation

Without stable care, orphaned children often move from one temporary home to another, living with relatives who are already struggling with poverty. These realities create long-term barriers that affect not only their childhood but also their future opportunities in life.

Our Response: Building Hope

Recognizing this urgent need, Helping Hands Uganda has begun the construction of a new and safer orphan home. This project aims to provide a stable and protective environment where children can live, grow, and regain the sense of security that every child deserves.

Construction Progress

35% Complete — We need your support to finish the home!

The new home will provide adequate sleeping areas, improved sanitation, and a safe space where children can study, play, and build friendships. Creating this environment is not only about constructing walls and roofs; it is about restoring dignity and stability in the lives of children who have already experienced significant loss and hardship.

What We Provide

Safe Shelter

A secure, loving home where children can sleep without fear and feel protected.

Nutritious Meals

Regular, healthy meals to support proper growth and development.

Clothing & Bedding

Clean clothing, bedding, and personal care items for every child.

Emotional Support

Caregivers who create a family-like environment where children feel valued, listened to, and supported.

Education Access

Opportunities to attend school and participate in educational activities.

Mentorship & Guidance

Helping children rebuild trust and develop a stronger sense of self-worth.

Supporting Children in Foster Families

In addition to the children living in the shelter, the organization also supports other orphaned children who are currently staying in foster homes with relatives or trusted families in the community. Through this combined approach, Helping Hands Uganda ensures that children who cannot be accommodated in the shelter still receive support and monitoring to safeguard their well-being.

"Despite the limitations, our caregivers continue to do everything possible to provide a sense of safety and belonging for the children. Every child deserves more than survival; they deserve the opportunity to grow in a nurturing environment where they can dream, learn, and become responsible members of society."

— Helping Hands Uganda Care Team

Our Child Protection Commitment

Helping Hands Uganda is committed to protecting children from abuse, exploitation, and neglect. The organization operates under a Child Protection and Safeguarding Policy to ensure the safety and well-being of all children under its care. All staff and volunteers undergo background checks and safeguarding training.

Our promise: Every child in our care is treated with respect, given a voice, and supported to pursue their dreams. We never label or stigmatize those we serve.
