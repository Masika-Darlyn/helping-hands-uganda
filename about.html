Who We Are

Helping Hands Uganda is a grassroots humanitarian nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting vulnerable children, empowering women and girls, and strengthening communities across Uganda.

Founded in 2022 in Katumba Village, Bundibugyo District, our organization was born from compassion and a commitment to stand with those facing poverty, abandonment, and limited opportunities. Today, we operate as a registered Community Based Organization (CBO) serving communities across Uganda through education support, livelihood development, orphan care, and community empowerment programs.

Our Story

Bukama Hakim

Founder & Chairperson

Helping Hands Uganda was born from the hardships of the COVID-19 pandemic and the lived experiences of its founder, Bukama Hakim. Born into a life that taught vulnerability firsthand, Bukama witnessed his mother struggle as a young wife in a polygamous household, eventually leaving to protect her children. By age seven, he knew what it felt like to be displaced, to struggle for stability, and to watch a mother fight tirelessly for her children's future.

I believe deeply that human beings must help fellow human beings. Every child deserves protection. Every girl deserves education. Every vulnerable person deserves dignity. This belief is the heart of Helping Hands Uganda.

In 2022, Bukama started a small gardening project, sharing harvests with vulnerable families. What began as a simple act of compassion grew into a tailoring initiative with five sewing machines, and eventually evolved into a comprehensive humanitarian organization. Today, Helping Hands Uganda cares for orphaned children, provides skills training, and works to build a future where hope is stronger than hardship.

Who We Serve

Helping Hands Uganda focuses on supporting the most vulnerable members of our communities:

Orphaned Children

Children who have lost parental care, needing shelter, love, and education

Vulnerable Children

Children facing neglect, poverty, or unstable home environments

School Dropouts

Youth who left school due to poverty, now seeking second chances

Single Mothers

Women struggling to provide for their children with limited resources

Women & Girls

Empowering through skills training and economic opportunities

Low-income Farmers

Supporting agricultural productivity and food security

Small Business Owners

Providing micro-loans through our SACCO initiative

Rural Communities

Improving access to clean water and basic services

Our Key Programs

Orphan Care & Shelter

Providing safe housing, nutritious meals, and emotional support for orphaned children. Currently constructing a new orphan home.

Education Support

Scholastic materials, tuition assistance, and school enrollment for vulnerable children.

Women Empowerment

Tailoring skills training and vocational programs for women and girls.

Community SACCO

Savings and credit initiative serving 20+ farmers and small business owners.

Water & Sanitation

Well construction and clean water access projects in rural communities.

Humanitarian Aid

Emergency support including clothing, bedding, and household necessities.

Our Commitment to Child Protection

Helping Hands Uganda is deeply committed to protecting children from abuse, exploitation, and neglect. We operate under a comprehensive Child Protection and Safeguarding Policy that ensures the safety and well-being of every child under our care. All staff and volunteers undergo background checks and safeguarding training.

Our principle: Every child has the right to safety, education, and a nurturing environment where they can thrive.
