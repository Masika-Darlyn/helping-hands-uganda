Making a Difference

At Helping Hands Uganda, we believe in creating lasting change through comprehensive, community-centered programs. Our projects address the root causes of vulnerability while providing immediate support to those who need it most. Each project is designed with sustainability in mind, ensuring that the impact continues long after the initial intervention.

Impact Story: Masika's Journey

"Through the generous support of a donor, Masika Ritah, one of the vulnerable children supported by Helping Hands Uganda, was successfully enrolled in school. This milestone has given her a chance to begin her educational journey and pursue the dreams that every child deserves to have."

Masika's story demonstrates the life-changing impact that donor support can have on the lives of vulnerable children. Today, she attends school regularly and dreams of becoming a nurse to help others in her community. Many other children are still waiting for the same opportunity.
