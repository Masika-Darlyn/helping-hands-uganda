✨ Helping Hands Uganda exists to break the cycle of poverty. Founded in 2022 after the COVID-19 pandemic exposed the deep struggles faced by rural communities, our mission is simple but powerful: restore dignity, opportunity, and hope to those who need it most.

Our Story

By Bukama Hakim, Founder

"Sometimes the deepest compassion is born from the hardest experiences. I was born into a life that taught me very early what vulnerability feels like. My mother was married at just 21 years old, had never attended school, and faced constant challenges. When I was seven years old, she made the difficult decision to leave the marriage..."

Every child deserves protection. Every girl deserves education. Every vulnerable person deserves dignity.
